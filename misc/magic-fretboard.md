# Magic Fretboard

Magic Fretboard is a separate project. It has nothing to do with NFTs or Arweave storage. It is an interactive guitar fretboard that runs entirely in the browser, and it is the best practice tool I have used.

## What It Does

### Scales and Chords

33 scale modes, from the major modes through harmonic and melodic minor, symmetric scales, and pentatonics.

Chord voicings come from four systems that all cover major, minor, augmented, diminished, sus2, and sus4:

* **CAGED** — the five open forms mapped across the whole neck
* **Open Triads** — spread voicings with every inversion
* **Fat Shapes** — compact stacked-fourths voicings
* **Magic Modes** — voicing suggestions ranked by diatonic fit and voice leading, cycled with the number keys

### Frame Sequencer

Build progressions frame by frame with Roman numeral analysis, secondary dominants, tritone subs, and pivot chords. Harmonic rhythm handles odd meters. Navigation markers cover Segno, D.S. al Coda, D.C. al Coda, and loops. Magic Voice Leading places voicings automatically and shifts them with the arrow keys.

### Layers

Up to four independent fretboard layers, each with its own instrument, tuning, voicings, playback mode, and melody strategy. Chord layers play together on strong beats.

### Melody Mode

Your chord frames become a palette for improvising. 18 strategies run from pure random to Intelligent+Advanced, which weights notes by chord tone, beat position, rhythmic density, and phrase direction, and remembers what you played at each beat so mistakes turn into recurring motifs.

Melody Maps let you export a template MIDI, write a melody in your DAW, import it back, and either play it exactly or blend it with intelligent fills. Modifiers add slides, anticipation, harmony, and vibrato. Decorations add arpeggio sweeps, full-chord hits, and pitch bends.

### Joy-Con Control

Two Nintendo Joy-Cons work as a physical melody instrument over WebHID. Shake to trigger notes, read from the IMU at ~800Hz. The left one handles rhythm, strategy, and note repeat. The right one handles expression, with analog pitch bend on the stick. The Joy-Cons vibrate at the pitch of the note you played.

### MIDI and DAW Sync

Clock sync so your DAW drives tempo and transport, note input as rhythm triggers, and note output for recording your melodies back into the DAW. Setup guides for Ableton, Logic Pro, and GarageBand are built in. Chrome and Edge only.

### Practice Tools

15 GM instrument sounds, a metronome with count-in, tap tempo, slow mode that holds a steady 4/4 across frames with different harmonic rhythms, and bass accompaniment on strong beats.

### Multiplayer

Real-time collaboration over WebRTC, no backend. Teaching mode mirrors the host's board to guests. Collaboration mode lets everyone edit with instant sync. Up to seven participants with invite links.

### Song Library and Lessons

Around 154 songs across 11 genres, searchable by Roman numerals, song names, or keywords, so you can see which famous songs share a progression. 33 built-in lessons with annotations, quizzes, and video embeds, plus support for importing your own `.mfl` lesson files.

### Instruments

Guitar in 6, 7, and 8 strings with 22 tunings, bass in 4 and 5, ukulele, mandolin, and banjo.

### Visuals and Export

Labels as scale degrees or note names, synesthesia mode for per-pitch colors, and 14 stackable visual effects with 15 retro palettes behind secret codes. Export the board as SVG or JPG, or save everything as an `.mfs` snapshot and drag it back in later.

Press **H** in the app for the full keyboard shortcut guide.
