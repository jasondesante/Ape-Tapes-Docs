# Stats & Achievements

Tracks site activity with tabs: general, songs, insights, achievements. Includes charts for top interactions.&#x20;

The Stats & Achievements page has lots of fun stats to see your activity on the site over time. It used to be two separate pages, and is now one page with four tabs. Open it from the wallet menu.

Everything on it is yours and stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

### General Stats

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-07-31 at 3.31.34 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

General Stats has over 80 stats across 10 different categories. The time-based ones count up in real time while you sit there.

The four stat blocks at the top cover general totals, extra computed stats, wallet stats, and contract stats. Under those are six activity categories:

* **Actions** — plays, pauses, skips, completes, next and previous clicks, every favorite and unfavorite
* **Metadata Creation** — files uploaded, bytes uploaded, manifests, song and contract metadata made
* **Playlist Activity** — playlists created, deleted, uploaded, and tokens added to them
* **Wizard Activity** — dialogues spoken, times you talked to the Wizard and the Hat
* **Blockchain Activity** — tokens minted, recycled, staked, masters created, promos created and claimed, paperwork filed
* **Visualizer Activity** — everything from avatars loaded and dances danced to your largest dance party and longest killing spree

### Song Stats

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-07-31 at 3.31.25 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

View your listening activity on the Song Stats tab. Every track you have played, in a sortable table, 15 per page.

Songs are grouped by audio file rather than by token, so the same track collected from three different contracts shows up once with its plays added together. Sort by any column, click a column again to flip the direction, and click a thumbnail to see the art full screen.

### Insights

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-07-31 at 3.32.04 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Insights tab shows you different leaderboards related to your activity around different subjects.  Most listened to track, most searched query, most common genre.  You can view the insights in a chart or list.

Time-based boards rank total time by artist, song, album, and year. Count-based boards rank genre, tags, year, chain, artist, contract name, and search queries. Each board toggles between a list and a chart, and each chart toggles between pie and bar.

Recently Played Lists sits at the bottom, showing the favorited users, labels, and playlists you played most recently.

### Achievements

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-07-31 at 3.32.31 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Achievements tab contains the list of all the achievements that you can unlock on the site. Currently there are 321 achievements to unlock, with a gamerscore and a completion percentage at the top.

Click a category to view the related achievements available to be unlocked.  More achievements will appear as you unlock them.

Achievements that come in a series show the levels you have unlocked plus the next locked one, so you can always see what you are working toward. Locked achievements show their requirement as a teaser and nothing else. Switch between grid and list view depending on how much detail you want.
