# Files

Everything under Create starts here: [The Audio Uploader](audio-uploader/) tags and uploads your audio, the [Arweave Uploads page](arweave-uploads-page.md) keeps track of your uploads, and the [File Picker](file-picker.md) connects your files to the rest of the tools.

The rule across all of them: your wallet signs every upload, and your files land on permanent storage nobody can take back. Not me, not anyone.
