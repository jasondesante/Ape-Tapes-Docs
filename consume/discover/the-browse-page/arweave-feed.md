# Arweave Feed

The Arweave Feed page is the tag search and the Time Machine in one. Search audio, playlists, and files using tags on Arweave, then roll back time and crate dig through everything you find.

You can reach it from the Browse page, or the "Start Digging" button on the welcome screen.

## Audio Search

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-07-12 at 5.07.36 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Open the filters to search audio by title, artist, genre, tag, ISRC, or uploader — including your own wallet. For files uploaded with The Contract Wizard, and all of Arweave.

## Playlist Search

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-07-12 at 5.08.06 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Search playlists by name, genre, tag, or creator. It's easy to start curating, and even easier to search once it's been curated.

## File Search

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-07-12 at 5.11.06 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Search any file on Arweave by uploader, owner address, or IPFS CID — or add custom tags in name:value form to search anything that's tagged. Uploads made with The Contract Wizard carry a full set of tags automatically.

## The Time Machine

The Time Machine is a magical device that lets you scroll back through time and crate dig through old files.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-07-12 at 5.18.58 AM.png>)

### How To Activate

Click the Time button and the thumbwheels appear. Spin the wheels to roll back through years, months, weeks, and days.

The Search button becomes Scan — click it to see what you found while traveling back through the ages.

Time Machine activated.

### How To Enjoy

Choose from the grid, list, or the fun 3d crate digging mode. The crate is always open — you can dig through any results, no time travel required. In the crate: arrows or WASD to flip through records, Enter to zoom and spin, Space to play.

Use the feed to browse all files on Arweave, or keep it on This Site's Files — uploads made with The Contract Wizard, for a higher probability of tracks with artwork.

Enjoy the art of the track on the cover of the record, or a randomly generated one to keep the vibes. &#x20;
